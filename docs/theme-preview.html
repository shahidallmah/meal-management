Usage rules

  • #52ab98 with white text fails contrast (2.8:1). Use it for fills, icons, toggles and badges; use #33806f when you need green text or a green button with white text.
  • White text on #2b6777 passes (6.3:1), so it's the default for primary buttons and the app bar.
  • Body text is a deep teal-ink #1e3a42, not pure black, so it sits with the palette.
  • Meal ON is green, meal OFF is grey (smoke). Days outside the editable window get a light blue-grey "locked" background.
  • Cost split: employee share in teal, company share in green, the same in every chart and report.
